# Karthikeya Machiraju

Quantum computing researcher at **PES University Bengaluru** (B.Tech CS/AI-ML) and **IIT Madras** (B.Sc Data Science).  
Research at the **Photonics & Quantum Technology Lab (PQTL)** under Dr. Kaustav Bhowmick.  
Leads **Q-Forest** (Quantum Computing Society) and serves as Research Head at **Equinox Space Tech Society**.

## Research Interests
Quantum simulation of quantum field theories · Lattice gauge theories · Non-equilibrium dynamics · Variational quantum algorithms · Floquet/many-body physics · Open quantum systems

## Selected Work
- **Schwinger Model VQE** — variational quantum simulation of 1+1D QED (targeting IEEE TQE)
- **Prethermal Time Crystals** — Floquet many-body physics in open quantum systems (targeting Phys. Rev. Research)
- **Holonomic Quantum Gates** — photonic waveguide implementation (submitted to QIP/Springer)
- **Quantum QSVM for QCD Observables** — quantum kernel + VQE methods applied to QCD ([repo](https://github.com/0Shunya0/quantum-qsvm-qcd))
